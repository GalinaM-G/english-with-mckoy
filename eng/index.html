about me

My name is Galina McKoy. I am a certified English teacher with many years of teaching experience.

For me, teaching is not just about explaining a topic. It is about understanding what is holding a student back and finding the right way to help them move forward. That is why I adapt explanations and practice to each student, their level and their goals.

I want English to become more than a set of rules — a practical tool that students can use with greater confidence and freedom..
